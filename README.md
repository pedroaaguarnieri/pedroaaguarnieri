## About

I work where the field meets the code. I have a technical degree in Agriculture and I'm completing Agronomy and Software Engineering, which lets me understand what the field actually needs and design the solution for it: from the system that organizes the operation to the data that arrives clean on the decision-maker's dashboard.

Day to day, I manage agricultural systems and build the bridges between them: integrations, data pipelines and automations that take manual work out of the way and give confidence to the number that reaches the decision-maker.

## What I build

**Integration & data**
- **Systems integration** — two-way sync between ERPs and field platforms, with identity matching across records, an error queue and a transaction history
- **Data engineering** — data warehouse on a medallion architecture (bronze, silver, gold), with orchestration, validation and reprocessing, pulling together dozens of heterogeneous sources
- **Telemetry & IoT** — gateways that receive TCP/UDP packets from trackers and scales, store the raw data and distribute it to integrations
- **Automation & reporting** — automatic generation of operational PDF reports from weighing data, telemetry and photos

**Agronomy & intelligence**
- **Agronomy applied to data** — pest development modeling, phytosanitary monitoring with geospatial data, and decision support for crop management
- **Computer vision & web** — license plate recognition (LPR) integrated with cameras and access controllers, and web ordering platforms with an admin area
- **AI in the workflow** — skills and routines for coding agents, and a personal knowledge base organized by AI

**Platform & backend**
- **Agricultural ERP** — design and development of an ERP for farm operations: crop seasons, inputs, field activity logging, costs and finance, with group-based permissions, an audit trail and soft deletes
- **APIs & backend** — asynchronous APIs with JWT authentication, schema-based input/output validation, access control by module and action, and Redis-backed caching and rate limiting

**Infrastructure & reliability**
- **Infrastructure & DevOps** — self-managed servers with Docker Compose, nginx and Cloudflare, HTTPS with automatic renewal, deploys controlled by GitHub Actions and scheduled updates
- **Continuity & security** — automatic cloud backups with a monthly restore test, access restricted through Cloudflare, and disk and certificate forecasting before they become a problem
- **Monitoring & observability** — metrics collection with Zabbix and Grafana dashboards, with WhatsApp alerts via n8n automations, to track operations in real time

> Most of this work is done for companies, so it lives in private repositories. Below is the project I can show.
